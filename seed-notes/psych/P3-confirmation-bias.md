# Confirmation bias

People tend to look for, notice, and remember information that fits what they
already believe, while overlooking or dismissing evidence that contradicts it.

This shows up as searching only for supporting examples, reading ambiguous facts in
a way that favors the existing view, and giving more weight to agreeable sources
than to challenging ones. The belief then feels increasingly well-supported even
when the full picture is mixed.

A practical counter is to deliberately ask what evidence would prove you wrong, and
to go looking for that evidence on purpose.
